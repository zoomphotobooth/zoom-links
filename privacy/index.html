Privacy Policy

Zoom Photobooth Co. · Ottawa, Ontario · Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Zoom Photobooth Co. collects when you contact us, request a quote or book an event, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill out one of our forms, including forms on Facebook and Instagram, send us an email or call us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, and details about your event such as the date, venue and number of guests.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • To plan, confirm and deliver your booking
  • To send you occasional news and offers by email, which you can unsubscribe from at any time

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We use trusted service providers to run our business, such as our booking system, email marketing service and website hosting. They handle your information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to respond to you, deliver your event and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.
