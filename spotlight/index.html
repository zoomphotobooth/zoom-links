How it works

Four taps from scan to shared.

  1. Scan or tap

    Customers open your QR code or link. It runs in the phone's browser, so there's nothing to download.

  2. Pick a look

    They choose your frame and a background: spotlight stage, neon, retro or Ottawa skyline.

  3. Snap

    They take the photo on their own phone and retake as often as they like.

  4. Share

    They send it by text or email or post it, with your logo and handle on every photo.

Questions

Good to know

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. The booth opens in the phone's web browser from your QR code or link.

Can I change the design later?

Yes. Always-On includes a new seasonal design every quarter, and Always-On Plus refreshes monthly. Ask us any time you have a promotion coming up.

Do I need a green screen?

No. Background scenes are applied to the photo automatically, so customers can be anywhere.

Who sets it up?

We do. Send us your logo and colours, and we design the frame, set up the booth and send you your QR code and print-ready artwork.
